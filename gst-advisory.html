Overview

What is GST Advisory?

GST Advisory covers the full lifecycle of Goods and Services Tax compliance — from initial registration and monthly return filing to reconciliation, refund claims and representation before tax authorities. With GST law evolving through frequent notifications and rate changes, businesses need ongoing expert guidance to stay compliant, claim eligible input tax credit, and avoid interest, penalties or blocked credit due to mismatches.

Common Questions

GST Advisory FAQs

Registration is mandatory once aggregate turnover crosses ₹40 lakh for goods (₹20 lakh in special category states) or ₹20 lakh for services, or earlier for specific categories like e-commerce sellers and inter-state suppliers.

GSTR-1 reports outward supplies (sales) in detail, while GSTR-3B is a summary return used to pay tax and claim input tax credit for the period.

Input tax credit can now only be claimed to the extent it appears in GSTR-2B; regular reconciliation prevents credit blockage, notices, and cash-flow strain from unclaimed credit.

Common triggers include exports under LUT, inverted duty structure (input tax rate higher than output), and excess cash ledger balance.

GSTR-9 is generally mandatory above a specified turnover threshold, with GSTR-9C (reconciliation statement) required above a higher threshold — we assess applicability each year.

Do not ignore it — notices carry strict response deadlines. We review the notice, prepare a documented reply, and represent your business before the authority if required.
